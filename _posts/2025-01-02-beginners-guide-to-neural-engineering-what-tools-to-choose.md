### Introduction
Starting an adventure in Human neuroscience can be very exciting. 
Let's be honest doing neuroscience research sounds a little bit like being in a science-fiction movie. A lot of neologism are made from the suffix "Neuro". 
There has been a resurgence of interest and activity around neuro-whatever 
businesses in the last few years. A good case in point is the current craze for so-called ‘brain training’ products (and for a lot of BS, pardon my french) 
it is a buzz word (though AI's/LLM/Generative AI's shadow lately). 
Media are all over the place when "brain-computer-interface" is mentioned. 
I heard many excited students sying they were going to cure Alzheimer's 
disease or Parkinson's or whatnot! 

Cute.

But things become serious and scary when it comes to to get our hands dirty 
dealing with real world neural data. And it can be very daunting for those 
who just got in. Where to start? How do I analyze the data? What programming
language? What package? I am going to talk about the modality I know the best:
Electroencephalography (EEG) and intracerebral EEG (sEEG).
